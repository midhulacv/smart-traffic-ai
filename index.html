Project Abstract

Traffic congestion is a major problem in urban and semi-urban areas. Traditional traffic signals use fixed timing intervals without considering the actual number of vehicles on each road.

This project proposes an AI-Based Smart Traffic Signal System that uses cameras, image processing and machine learning to detect and estimate vehicle density on different roads.

Python and OpenCV are proposed for vehicle detection and image processing. Based on the detected traffic density, an Arduino Nano or compatible microcontroller controls traffic signal LEDs and adjusts green-light duration.

The concept aims to reduce unnecessary waiting time, improve traffic flow and minimize fuel consumption and vehicle emissions.

Problem Statement

Fixed Timing

Traditional signals operate with fixed intervals regardless of traffic conditions.

Long Waiting

Vehicles on congested roads may experience unnecessary waiting times.

Fuel Wastage

Prolonged idling can increase fuel consumption and vehicle emissions.

Proposed System

The proposed system analyzes traffic images or videos to estimate vehicle density and allocate suitable green-light durations.
