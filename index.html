Project Overview

The problem, proposed solution and objectives.

The Problem

Conventional traffic signals generally use fixed time intervals without considering actual vehicle density. This can waste green time on less crowded roads while congested roads experience long waits.

Proposed Solution

The proposed system uses a camera or pre-recorded traffic video. Vehicle detection and density estimation help determine suitable green-light durations for each road.

Project Objectives

  • Detect and count vehicles.
  • Classify traffic as low, medium or high.
  • Adjust green-light timing based on density.
  • Reduce unnecessary waiting and idling.

Expected Outcomes

  • Improved traffic-flow efficiency.
  • Reduced unnecessary waiting time.
  • Potential reduction in fuel consumption.
  • Lower emissions from prolonged idling.
